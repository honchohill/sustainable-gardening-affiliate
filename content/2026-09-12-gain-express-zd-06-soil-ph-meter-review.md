## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Knowing your soil's pH is the foundation of chemical-free gardening. When pH is in the right range (6.0–7.0 for most vegetables), plants absorb nutrients naturally, eliminating the need for synthetic soil amendments. The ZD-06 reads without disposable chemical kits or lithium batteries — its analog design means zero waste, zero calibrations, and zero ongoing costs. In a world where soil health is the linchpin of regenerative gardening, a tool that tells you exactly where your soil stands is the first investment that pays for itself.
