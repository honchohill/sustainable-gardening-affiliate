## Quick Verdict

The SCD Probiotics All Seasons Indoor Composter is the only mainstream indoor system that lets you compost meat, dairy, bones, and oily foods alongside your regular vegetable scraps — truly everything from the kitchen. Using anaerobic fermentation (bokashi), it turns food waste into pre-compost in days instead of months. At ~$65-87 depending on kit variant, it's pricier than a basic countertop bin, but the ability to compost everything is unmatched. Best for zero-waste households, families generating mixed waste, and anyone frustrated by the "no meat or dairy" rules of traditional composting.

## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Traditional composting has limitations: you can't add meat, dairy, oily foods, or bones without attracting pests and creating odors. Most households toss these items into the trash, missing a huge chunk of recoverable organic material. Bokashi anaerobic fermentation sidesteps this problem entirely. By sealing food waste in an airtight bucket with inoculated bran, beneficial microbes ferment everything — even chicken bones eventually dissolve. The result is a nutrient-preserving method that creates less CO₂ than aerobic decomposition while retaining more plant-available nutrients in the final product.

- **Anaerobic fermentation** — The airtight seal and bokashi bran create an anaerobic environment where beneficial microbes (Lactobacillus) break down waste without oxygen, eliminating the odors and pests of open composting.

- **Includes bokashi bran** — Ships with 1-4 lbs of All Seasons Bokashi (fermented wheat and rice bran) to kickstart fermentation. You'll need to top up replenishment bags.
- **Made in Kansas City, USA** — Proudly US-made with a small carbon footprint and science-backed probiotic technology.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros:**
- Compost literally everything from the kitchen — no sorting, no restrictions
- Odor-free when sealed correctly; the airtight lid is genuinely airtight
- Produces compost tea as a bonus liquid fertilizer for garden plants
- Compact footprint fits under the sink or in a pantry corner
- Fermentation is fast — food is ready to bury in 2-4 weeks vs. months for aerobic compost
- Retains more nutrients than aerobic composting (fermentation preserves rather than releasing them)
- Kid- and pet-safe bokashi bran doubles as a natural odor neutralizer in trash cans, diaper pails, litter boxes

**Cons:**
- Requires ongoing purchase of bokashi bran (~$25-30 per gallon replenishment)
- The fermented product isn't finished compost — it must be buried in soil or added to an outdoor bin to fully decompose
- Initial learning curve: balance of bran, packing technique, and tea drainage takes practice
- Some users report the drain spigot can clog if not monitored regularly
- 5-gallon size fills fast in large households; you may want to run two buckets
- Price at ~$65-87 is higher than a stainless steel countertop bin
