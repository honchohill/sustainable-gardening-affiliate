## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Water waste is one of the biggest hidden impacts of home gardening. Traditional hose watering can waste up to 50% of water to evaporation, runoff, and overspray. Drip irrigation delivers moisture directly to the root zone, cutting water use by 70% compared to standard hose methods. The TRJZWA takes this further by making the entire system solar-powered, meaning zero grid electricity is needed to run the pump. For gardeners trying to minimize their environmental footprint without sacrificing plant health, this is exactly the kind of tool that makes a measurable difference.
