## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Wooden compost bins are the gold standard for material sustainability. Unlike plastic tumblers that shed microplastics or steel bins that eventually rust, cedar ages gracefully—developing a silvery patina while staying structurally sound for years. The open-bottom design lets earthworms and beneficial microbes migrate directly from the soil into the compost pile, kickstarting decomposition naturally without additives or inoculants.

Greens Fence sources cedar from managed forests in North America, meaning every board is traceable to sustainable forestry practices. No chemicals, no treatments, no synthetic finishes—just wood that breaks down when its life is done.
