## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Outdoor residential watering accounts for up to 30% of home water use, and much of it is wasted from overwatering, forgotten hoses, and ignoring weather conditions. Smart controllers that use local weather data to skip unnecessary cycles typically cut outdoor water use by 20–50%. The Rachio brings this proven water-saving technology to hose-based setups — drip irrigation, oscillating sprinklers, soaker hoses — without plumbing into an in-ground sprinkler system.

## Key Features

- **Weather-Based Rain Skips**: Rachio pulls hyper-local weather data and automatically skips scheduled watering when rain is forecast. You can set a rain skip threshold from 1/16-inch up to 3/4-inch of expected rainfall. The system checks forecasts both 12 hours and 1 hour before each run, making it materially more reliable than competing timers.
- **Easy Faucet Install**: The valve is battery-powered (2xAA, not included) and screws directly onto standard hose bibs. No plumber, no electrician, no in-ground backbox. Dimensions are compact at approximately 6.81 x 5.82 x 3.62 inches, weighing about 12 ounces.
- **Expandable Hub System**: One Rachio Wi-Fi Hub supports up to eight valves, so a multi-faucet property needs only one hub. You can pair the vegetable garden, patio drip lines, and lawn sprinkler under a single app. The advertised wireless range is 200 feet, verified in independent testing.
- **Built-in Flow Monitoring**: Integrated flow meter tracks actual water usage, so you can see how many gallons each zone consumed. The app alerts you to unusual flow patterns that might indicate leaks or blocked emitters.
- **Smart Home Integration**: Works with Alexa, Google Home, IFTTT, and Apple HomeKit. Schedule runs by sunrise/sunset rather than fixed clock times, which matches how watering recommendations actually work.
- **Valve Memory Storage**: Each valve stores up to 400 scheduled runs locally. If your Wi-Fi goes down, watering continues uninterrupted — only the app loses visibility.
