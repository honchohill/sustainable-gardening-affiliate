### Cons
- **Water can heat in direct sun** — in hot Southern climates, the water inside can get uncomfortably warm for tree roots. Fill in the evening or cover with shade cloth to mitigate.
- **Potential pest habitat** — in humid climates, the dark space between bag and trunk can attract pill bugs or earwigs. Check periodically in those environments.
- **Not for long-term permanent use** — extended use can encourage root circling within the root ball. Use primarily during establishment (first 1–2 years), then transition to drip-line watering.
- **Awkward on trees with low branches** — the bag needs clearance of at least 25 inches from ground to first branches.
- **Price per unit** — at $29, it's more expensive than generic knockoffs, but the quality gap is real.

### Eco-conscious tips

- In hot climates, fill the bag in the evening to keep water temperature in a root-friendly range.

- Use collected rainwater from a rain barrel to fill the bag — fully closes the water sustainability loop.
- After the establishment period (1–2 years), transition watering focus to the drip line and remove the bag to prevent root circling.
