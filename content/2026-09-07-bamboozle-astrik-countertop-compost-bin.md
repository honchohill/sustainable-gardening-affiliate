# Bamboozle Astrik Countertop Compost Bin: The Composter That Composts

The **Bamboozle Astrik Countertop Compost Bin** ($50 on Amazon, available in multiple colors) is a 1-gallon indoor scrap collector made from Astrik—a proprietary 10% plant-based composite that replaces petroleum plastics. Good Housekeeping, Food Network, and The Kitchn have all named it a top pick for 2026, and at 4.4 stars with 481+ Amazon reviews, buyers are on board.

## Quick Verdict

Best-looking countertop composter on the market, made from a genuinely biodegradable alternative to plastic. Hemp-based carbon filters keep odors locked away for weeks. At $50 it is pricier than stainless steel options, but you are paying for materials that return to the soil when their life is done.

## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Every countertop composter starts you on the path. By keeping a convenient bin on the counter, you actually collect scraps instead of tossing them with regular trash. The Bamboozle takes it further: the bin itself is made from a plant-based material designed to break down in industrial composting facilities at end-of-life, rather than sitting in a landfill for centuries like typical plastic bins.

It is a collection bin, not a composter per se—you still need an outdoor pile, worm bin, or municipal drop-off—but it removes the biggest barrier to composting: laziness. If the bin is visible and accessible, you compost.

## Key Features

- **1-gallon capacity**: Lasts about a week for a family of four before emptying.
- **Astrik material**: 10% plant-based PLA (polylactic acid) composite derived from corn starch and sugarcane. Biodegradable at industrial composting facilities.
- **Hemp-based odor filters**: Two included filters last 2-3 months each, replacing traditional carbon with a plant fiber alternative.
- **Ventilated lid**: Lets gases escape during fermentation while the filter blocks smells.
- **Dishwasher safe**: One-piece molded body cleans easily. No rust unlike stainless steel competitors.
- **Bamboo handle**: Detachable handle for carrying to outdoor composter or drop-off. Rotates out of the way during use.
- **Dimensions**: 7.25"L x 7.25"W x 9"H. Lightweight and compact.

## Sustainable Angle

The Astrik material is the real story here. While most "eco-friendly" compost bins are still made from virgin plastic or stainless steel, the Bamboozle uses a PLA composite derived from renewable crops. When the bin eventually wears out—and it is durable enough that this is years away—it can be processed at industrial composting facilities where it breaks down into water, carbon dioxide, and organic matter rather than fragmenting into microplastics.

Even the odor filters are plant-based hemp, a step beyond the mined activated carbon found in competing bins. The bamboo handle is sustainably harvested and replaces the metal or plastic handles common on other models. Yes, the filters are a recurring cost, but at ~$2.50 each with a 2-3 month lifespan, the trade-off for plant-based materials is reasonable.

## Final Thoughts

The Bamboozle Astrik is not the cheapest kitchen composter. It will not be the cheapest option in any comparison table. What it is, is the most authentic: a composting product whose own materials follow the same decomposing cycle as the food scraps it holds. The design is attractive, the odor control works, and the dishwasher-safe body makes it genuinely low-maintenance. If sustainability matters more than savings, this is the one.
