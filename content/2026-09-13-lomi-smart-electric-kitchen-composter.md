## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Average American households send 30% of their food to landfills, where it rots anaerobically and produces methane—a greenhouse gas 80 times more potent than CO₂ over 20 years. The Lomi intercepts that waste stream at the counter. Instead of a smelly bin that sits until the next trash haul, your scraps become a dry, crumbly amendment ready for potting mix, garden beds, and worm bins. It turns food waste from a disposal problem into a soil-building resource, and it does it without backyard space, worms, or waiting six months for a pile to mature.
