## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Seed starting generates a staggering amount of disposable plastic waste. Billions of nursery trays, cell packs, and six-packs end up in landfills every growing season — and that's assuming you even bother recycling them. Peat-based pots are the alternative most gardeners reach for, but peat harvesting itself is ecologically destructive. Peatlands store an estimated 30% of the world's soil carbon, and draining peat bogs to scoop out raw material releases gigatons of CO₂ each year. Buying peat pots, even biodegradable ones, indirectly funds that extraction.

Daniel's Plants solved the problem by using only one ingredient: spruce fibers from forestry byproducts. No peat, no fillers, no adhesives, no PFAS. The pots are OMRI-listed, making them safe and approved for any organic gardening operation. Roots grow cleanly through the porous walls, eliminating transplant shock entirely.
