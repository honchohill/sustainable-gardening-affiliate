## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Composting at home diverts up to 30% of household waste from landfills — and rotting food in landfills generates methane, a greenhouse gas 28× more potent than CO₂. The problem with traditional compost tumblers? Assembly friction. Most require 50+ bolts and nuts, turning what should be an eco-friendly purchase into a frustrating afternoon project. THE Growing solved this with pre-molded internal nuts locked into the resin panels, meaning you just screw and go. The result is actually getting composting started instead of abandoning the box.
