This is one of those products that earns its place in a sustainable garden through prevention, not remediation. Here's why it matters:

**Eliminates herbicide dependence.** Glyphosate and other synthetic herbicides show up consistently in waterways, and the EPA has documented health concerns around repeated exposure. A physical barrier removes that exposure entirely — no chemicals enter your soil or the broader ecosystem.

**Preserves soil biology.** Unlike herbicides that create chemical dead-zones, this fabric lets air, water, and nutrients reach the soil surface. Earthworms and beneficial microbes remain active below the barrier. Over the long run, your soil stays living and productive.

**Reduces labor and emissions.** Less time pulling weeds means fewer gas-powered trimmers and mowers running around the garden edge. For larger properties, the 400 sq ft per roll adds up quickly and cuts weed-maintenance labor by 60–80%.

### Eco-conscious tips for this product
