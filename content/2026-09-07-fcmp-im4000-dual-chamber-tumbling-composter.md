## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Composting diverts food waste from landfills where it generates methane—a greenhouse gas 25x more potent than CO2. The IM4000 lets you process kitchen scraps, yard waste, and paper products into nutrient-dense soil amendment in as little as two weeks. It is made entirely from post-consumer recycled plastic, so buying one actually closes the loop on materials already in circulation.

The dual-chamber design keeps a continuous composting cycle going—half the bin finishes while the other half fills. No downtime, no waiting for a single batch to cure before you can add more scraps.
