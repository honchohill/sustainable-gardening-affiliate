# Back to the Roots Self-Watering Terracotta Olla Pot Review (Pack of 3)

## Quick Verdict

The Back to the Roots Olla Pot Pack of 3 brings a 4,000-year-old irrigation technique into modern gardens: unglazed terracotta pots buried in the soil that slowly seep water directly to plant roots through microporous clay walls. Each pot holds about 700 mL, covers roughly 1.5 feet in diameter, and delivers consistent moisture for about a week per fill. No electricity, no timers, no plastic tubing, no pumps — just clay, water, and capillary action. For water-conscious gardeners, this is one of the most elegant and genuinely sustainable irrigation solutions available.

## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Water scarcity affects more gardeners than most realize. Traditional surface watering — sprinklers, hoses, overhead drip — can waste 30–70% of applied water to evaporation, runoff, and weed absorption. Olla irrigation eliminates that waste entirely: water moves from the pot only into soil that's actually drier than the reservoir, creating a self-regulating system that adapts to weather, soil type, and plant demand in real time. It's the kind of low-tech, zero-carbon efficiency that the sustainability movement desperately needs more of. For small-scale gardeners, containers, raised beds, and pollinator patches, a pack of three covers a significant area without any infrastructure or ongoing energy use.
