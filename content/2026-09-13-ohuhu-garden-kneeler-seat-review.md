## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Sustainable gardening isn't just about what you put in the soil — it's about whether you can keep putting yourself in the garden. Kneeling on hard ground for hours destroys knees. Once knee surgery ends your gardening career, the garden goes fallow and all that effort is lost. A good kneeler extends your ability to garden for decades. The Ohuhu specifically uses EVA foam — a denser, longer-lasting alternative to cheap polyurethane foam that breaks down in a season.
