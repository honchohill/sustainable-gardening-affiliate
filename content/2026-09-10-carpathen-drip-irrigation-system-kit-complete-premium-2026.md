## Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Water waste is one of the biggest hidden costs of home gardening. Traditional sprinklers can lose up to 80% of water to evaporation, wind drift, and runoff. Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the root zone, cutting water use by 30–80% compared to overhead sprinkler systems. For anyone committed to sustainable home gardening, installing a drip system is one of the highest-impact upgrades you can make — it immediately reduces your water footprint while keeping plants healthier and reducing fungal diseases caused by wet foliage.
