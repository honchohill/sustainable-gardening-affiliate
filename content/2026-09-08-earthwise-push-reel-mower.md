## Sustainable Angle

Switching from gas to reel mowing is one of the single highest-impact changes a homeowner can make for their carbon footprint. A typical gas mower emits 5–10 lbs of CO₂ per hour of use, plus unburned hydrocarbons, carbon monoxide, and nitrogen oxides. Over a 10-year lifespan, that adds up to hundreds of pounds of emissions—and the oil changes, fuel containers, and eventual disposal of the engine add more waste. The reel mower has none of that. Zero fuel means zero extraction, zero transport emissions, zero toxic runoff. The clean scissor cut also reduces plant stress, meaning less fertilization and fewer chemical treatments over the life of the lawn.
