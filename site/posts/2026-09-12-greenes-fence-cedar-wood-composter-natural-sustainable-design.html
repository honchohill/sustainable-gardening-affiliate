Its slatted design provides excellent natural airflow, the expandable capacity grows with your composting needs, and it blends into any garden landscape far better than a black plastic bin ever could. Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Most compost bins are made from virgin or recycled plastics that eventually degrade under UV exposure, releasing microplastics into the soil they're supposed to enrich. The Greenes Fence Cedar Composter takes the opposite approach: it's built from a natural, biodegradable material that was sustainably harvested. Western Red Cedar contains natural oils that resist rot and insect damage without chemical treatments, meaning no toxic preservatives are leaching into your compost. When the bin eventually reaches the end of its life — likely 10–15 years with basic care — the wood can be composted or repurposed. That full lifecycle, from harvest to decomposition, is what real sustainability looks like.
