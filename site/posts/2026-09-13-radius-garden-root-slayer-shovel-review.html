Key Features

  • Patented inverted V-shaped blade: Unlike traditional flat shovel edges, the V-tip concentrates downward force into a point that penetrates compacted soil and slices roots on contact. It's the same principle as a chisel versus a flat bar.
  • Root-cutting serrated edges: Both sides of the blade feature saw-tooth serrations that slice through roots during the rocking motion of digging. You don't need to reach for a hatchet when you hit a thick root ball.
  • Powder-coated carbon steel blade: At 2.5mm thick (just above 13 gauge), the blade handles root-heavy soil without bending. The powder coating resists rust, so you don't need to oil or store it inside — another point for real-world sustainability.
  • Natural Radius O-Handle Grip: Radius Garden's patented grip has four times the contact surface of a standard D-handle. It distributes pressure across the whole hand instead of concentrating it in the palm, dramatically reducing hand and wrist fatigue during extended digging sessions.
  • 44.5-inch overall length: Provides good leverage without being unwieldy. The 3.5-inch forward step lets you put real foot pressure behind the blade without the shaft twisting.
  • 5.4 lbs weight: On the heavier side for garden shovels, but the weight is what gives it cutting power and staying power in tough soil.

This shovel is for gardeners who regularly encounter root systems underground. If you're dividing perennials, transplanting shrubs, installing irrigation trenches, or planting in soil with established tree root networks, the Root Slayer pays for itself on the first day.
