Installation Is Simple

Place the bag around the trunk with zippers on the uphill side. Wrap both sides and zip from bottom to top. Lift the fill tag at the top, insert your hose, and fill to 1/4 capacity first, then gently lift the black straps to expand the bottom before filling to the desired level. Walk away and let it empty naturally over several hours.

Cons

Eco-conscious tips

  • In hot climates, fill the bag in the evening to keep water temperature in a root-friendly range.
  • Store the bag upside down to dry between seasons, extending its usable life significantly.
  • Use collected rainwater from a rain barrel to fill the bag — fully closes the water sustainability loop.
  • After the establishment period (1–2 years), transition watering focus to the drip line and remove the bag to prevent root circling.
