Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Biochar is literally one of the most sustainable garden amendments on the planet. It captures carbon permanently in the soil — studies show biochar carbon persists for hundreds to thousands of years. Unlike compost that breaks down and returns to the atmosphere, biochar stays. It also improves water retention in dry climates, reduces the need for synthetic fertilizers by holding nutrients in the root zone, and rebuilds degraded soils. The Char Bliss version uses wood waste that would otherwise go to landfill, diverting it into a carbon-negative soil amendment.

Sustainable Angle

This is carbon farming for your backyard. Biochar from wood waste performs three sustainability functions simultaneously: (1) it sequesters carbon that would otherwise release CO₂ from rotting wood in landfills, (2) it permanently stores that carbon in soil where it lasts centuries, and (3) it reduces ongoing fertilizer need by holding nutrients in the root zone.
