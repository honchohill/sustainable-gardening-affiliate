Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Composting is arguably the single most impactful practice a home gardener can adopt. Food scraps and yard waste account for roughly 30% of what ends up in landfills — methane-producing waste that could instead become the rich, living soil your garden craves. An 80-gallon bin can process a family's weekly kitchen scraps plus leaves and lawn clippings, dramatically cutting landfill contributions while feeding your garden for free. This isn't just about growing better tomatoes — it's about closing the loop between your kitchen and your garden soil.
