Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Synthetic fertilizers are energy-intensive to produce (Haber-Bosch nitrogen fixation alone consumes 1–2% of global energy) and create toxic runoff that dead zones form in coastal waters. Nature Sustainers uses slow-release organic sources that feed the soil food web first, then the plants. Each application builds soil biology instead of depleting it. For a sustainable garden, switching from conventional NPK pellets to OMRI-certified organic food is one of the fastest wins—reducing chemical input, protecting pollinators, and improving soil structure over time.

Sustainable Angle

Switching to organic fertilizer is a direct vote against industrial nitrogen fixation. Nature Sustainers' ingredient list—bone meal, cottonseed, kelp, gypsum—relies on naturally occurring nutrients that break down through microbial activity rather than chemical dissolution. This means the nutrients cycle through the soil food web, enriching the soil structure and carbon content with each application instead of depleting it. Over a full season, one 8-lb bag replaces 3–4 applications of synthetic fertilizer, each of which required petroleum-derived ammonia to manufacture. The OMRI seal guarantees no banned substances, so pollinators nearby and groundwater downstream are protected.
