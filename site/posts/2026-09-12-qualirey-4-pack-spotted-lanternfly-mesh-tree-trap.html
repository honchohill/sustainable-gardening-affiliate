Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Spotted lanternflies are one of the most destructive invasive pests in the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic United States. They sap tree vitality, attract secondary infections, and threaten vineyards, hardwood forests, and home gardens alike. Conventional treatment usually means broad-spectrum insecticides that also kill pollinators, birds, and soil organisms. The Qualirey mesh trap offers a mechanical alternative: it physically captures lanternflies as they climb tree trunks, without using poisons, baits, or sticky adhesives that trap birds and beneficial insects. In a garden where sustainability starts with protecting the broader ecosystem, this is the kind of targeted tool that makes a genuine difference.
