Why This Matters for Sustainable Gardening

Average American households send 30% of their food to landfills, where it rots anaerobically and produces methane—a greenhouse gas 80 times more potent than CO₂ over 20 years. The Lomi intercepts that waste stream at the counter. Instead of a smelly bin that sits until the next trash haul, your scraps become a dry, crumbly amendment ready for potting mix, garden beds, and worm bins. It turns food waste from a disposal problem into a soil-building resource, and it does it without backyard space, worms, or waiting six months for a pile to mature.

Sustainable Angle

The Lomi's biggest impact is methane avoidance. Every pound of food scrap processed indoors instead of sent to a landfill diverts roughly 1.6 lbs of CO₂-equivalent emissions. If you run a cycle every other day—as a family of four likely would—that's roughly 1,500 lbs of CO₂-e saved annually from compost alone. The B-Corp certification behind Purosys means the manufacturing supply chain also meets labor, environmental, and governance standards. The coconut-based filters are sustainably sourced and replaceable rather than disposable. The output enriches garden soil, reducing the need for manufactured potting mixes packaged in non-recyclable plastic bags.
